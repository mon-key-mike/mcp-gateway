## Positioning

Doodle Wood is a hand-drawn **pyrography studio** — sketchbook doodles burned
permanently into wood. Not a woodworking shop that added cute art. Doodle-first.

Beliefs: imperfection is the point; the material talks back; made in front of
people, not just for them; weird is a feature.

## Homepage

**Hero headline:** Doodles that escaped the notebook and grew into wood.

**Subhead:** Hand-drawn monkeys, mushrooms, and sacred geometry — burned permanently
into pendants, signs, and one-of-one relics. No two alike.

**CTAs:** Shop the Collection · Watch a Piece Get Burned

**Intro:** Every doodle starts the same way — bored in class, waiting on hold, sitting
by a fire with nothing to do but scribble. Most die in the margins. Doodle Wood rescues
them. We take the fire to the doodle instead of the doodle to a printer — no vinyl, no
vector-perfect edges, no assembly line.

**How it works:**
1. *It starts as a doodle* — loose sketchbook line work, never traced.
2. *The wood talks back* — grain lines become rivers, knots become eyes.
3. *Fire makes it permanent* — hand-burned, one at a time, often live at a booth near you.

**Closing band:** *Wild ideas. Solid wood.* — commission a custom piece, grab one from
the collection, or catch us live-burning at the next festival. Button: Start a Custom Order.
