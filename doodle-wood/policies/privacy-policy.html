Privacy Policy

This policy covers shop.doodle-wood.com (our store) and explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Order information — name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number, and what you bought. Needed to fulfil the order.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Email list — only if you opt in. Unsubscribe any time from the link in any email.
  • Workshop and commission enquiries — what you tell us in the enquiry, so we can quote and schedule.
  • Device and usage data — standard analytics: pages viewed, referring site, browser and device type.

What we do with it

Fulfil orders, arrange shipping, answer your questions, and — if you opted in — tell you about new pieces and festival dates. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Shopify — powers this store and processes orders on our behalf.
  • Payment processors — to take payment.
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order.
  • Email provider — if you are on the list.

We may also disclose information where legally required.

The brand site is separate

Our brand site at doodle-wood.com is a different system with its own email capture. Signing up there does not create a store account here, and vice versa. If that changes we will update this policy.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA.

Retention

Order records are kept as long as needed for tax and accounting purposes. Marketing contacts are kept until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

This store uses cookies to keep your cart working and to understand how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser, but the cart will stop working.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page.
