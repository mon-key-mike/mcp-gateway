Returns & Refunds

Every Doodle Wood piece is hand-burned one at a time. We want you to love yours — but because these are made by hand, please read how that changes things.

Stock items — 14 days

Pendants, stickers, patches, mugs, signs and wood slices bought from the collection can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are unused and in original condition. You cover return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or wrong.

What is not a defect

Wood is a natural material and fire is an imprecise tool. The following are characteristics of the craft, not faults, and are not grounds for return:

  • Grain, knot and colour variation between pieces
  • Burn depth and line weight varying slightly from the product photo
  • Minor asymmetry in hand-drawn line work

Each listing photo shows a representative piece, not the exact one you will receive, unless the listing says otherwise.

Custom commissions

Commissions are made to your brief and cannot be resold. Deposits are non-refundable once work has begun. If you cancel before we start burning, the deposit is refunded in full minus any materials already bought. We send a proof for approval before the piece is burned — once you approve the proof, the piece is final.

Workshop seats

Workshop seats are refundable up to 48 hours before the session. Inside 48 hours the seat is transferable to another person or another date, but not refundable — the blank and the bench are already committed. If we cancel a session, you get a full refund.

Damaged in transit

Send a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will replace or refund it, no return shipping needed. Wood ships well but it is not indestructible.

How to start a return

Email us with your order number and, for damage claims, a photo. We reply to returns within 2 business days.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights.
