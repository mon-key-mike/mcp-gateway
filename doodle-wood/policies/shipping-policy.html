Shipping

Production time

Pieces are burned to order unless the listing shows stock on hand. Typical timings:

  • Stickers, patches, mugs — ship within 1–3 business days
  • Pendants and wood slices — 3–7 business days
  • Signs and nameplates — 5–10 business days
  • Custom commissions — quoted per piece, typically 2–4 weeks including proof approval

Festival season slows things down. If we are out vending, production times extend and we will say so on the storefront.

Where we ship

United States. International shipping is not enabled yet — if you want a piece shipped outside the US, email us and we will quote it manually.

Rates

Calculated at checkout by weight and destination. Wood signs are heavier than they look; the rate reflects that.

Tracking

You get a tracking number by email when the piece ships. Once it is with the carrier, delivery timing is theirs, not ours.

Packaging

Pieces ship in recyclable, plastic-free packaging. Pendants ship with the cord attached in a small kraft box.
